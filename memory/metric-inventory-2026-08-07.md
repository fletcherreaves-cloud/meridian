## The Phase 1 gap

**29 metrics are described but have no auto-first resolution chain**, and are not single-source by design. They resolve from one hard-coded source, so they get no freshest-wins behaviour and no fallback — this is the population the recurring "blank tile / manual-only" bugs come from.
